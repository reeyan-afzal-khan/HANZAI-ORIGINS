How it works

Every question carries a sensitivity from 1 to 5 and a stage from icebreaker to intimate. You start on safe ground. After each one you tap deeper or lighter and the deck moves exactly one step — never further. Getting past sensitivity 3 asks you to confirm the other person is up for it, which with somebody you met an hour ago is the whole point.

Every card is HSK-aware. Its Chinese is cut into words and coloured by level; the HSK n chip is the level the question is readable at — the lowest that covers nine words in ten. Tap any word for its card and stroke order, and add it to your review deck from the study panel. Readable at HSK n above the decks keeps every run to questions you can already read.

On a phone: swipe the card right to go deeper, left to go lighter, up to skip. On a keyboard: ← → to change level, space for the next question, S to save, C to copy it — press ? for the rest.
